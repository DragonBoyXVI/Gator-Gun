
## Pregame
- Load profile?
- Click Play
- Choose gamemode (story, endless, sandbox, etc)
- Choose map
- Choose loadout
## Main game
- prepare defenses
- defend base from enemies
- spend gained resources on upgrades
- Repeat until lose or win condition is met
## Postgame
- story is progressed (if relivant)
- player is awarded meta currency/unlocks
- player may save score to a local leaderboard for that map
